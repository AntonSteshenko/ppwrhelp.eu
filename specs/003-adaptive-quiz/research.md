# Research and decisions — 2026-11-11

- **OpenRouter**: Its Chat Completions API accepts `response_format.type=json_schema`. Endpoint capability is provider-specific; `provider.require_parameters=true` excludes endpoints that ignore this parameter. The public endpoint listing for `google/gemini-2.5-flash` returned HTTP 200 and three Google AI Studio endpoints, all listing both `structured_outputs` and `response_format` among supported parameters on 2026-11-11. This verifies advertised capability, not an authenticated completion or strict-schema behavior. References: https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/structured-outputs and https://openrouter.ai/docs/api/api-reference/endpoints/list-endpoints.
- **Law**: Regulation (EU) 2025/40, OJ L 22 January 2025, official version at https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/40/oj. The selected Articles 1–2, 15 and 24 were used for the first corpus. Article 24's date depends on implementing acts, so it is not treated as an unconditional due date.
- **Guidance**: Commission Notice 2026/C 3084 at https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52026XC03084 and the Commission packaging page at https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/packaging-waste_en. Guidance is identified separately from binding text. National implementation details are outside initial coverage.
- **Hosting and cost**: Coolify is the target. Redis and distributed rate limiting were explicitly deferred. The application caps body size, history and attempts, but does not claim these are a distributed abuse limit; production needs external traffic and provider spending controls before promotion.
